01

Workforce Management App

One platform for organizations in Ethiopia to run attendance, shifts, leave and performance, in English or Amharic.

Team project · 3 people In active development · since May 2025

My role: Founder and product lead. I built the Company Admin and Super Admin web app frontends.

Problem

Organizations in Ethiopia need one place to see who clocked in, where and when, and to manage shifts, leave and performance goals for staff who often work in Amharic.

Approach

A multi-tenant platform where each organization gets its own workspace. It covers geofenced clock-in/out, shifts, leave management, attendance tracking, OKRs, performance review cycles, notifications and reports, with Amharic language support. I set the product direction and built the two admin web apps: Company Admin for each organization and Super Admin for running the platform across tenants.

Public Parks Platform

Reservations and on-site enforcement for public recreation facilities in Addis Ababa: one backend, three apps.

[CONFIRM: Team or Individual (+ team size)] Backend nearly complete · frontend in progress

My role: [ADD ROLE]

Problem

Public recreation facilities in Addis Ababa need a way for residents to book them, for staff to enforce bookings on site, and for the city to oversee it all.

Approach

Three clients (a resident booking app, a staff enforcement app and a city admin dashboard) share one NestJS backend that uses Drizzle ORM. The backend is built spec-first: the required behavior went into a written checklist before any code, and the backend is built and tested against that checklist with Vitest.

Result / Status

Backend nearly complete; frontend in progress.
